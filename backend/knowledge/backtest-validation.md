# Backtest validation

Reject experiments with look-ahead, leakage, survivorship bias, missing costs, weak baselines,
unstable parameters, or inadequate regime coverage. Use time-aware splits, walk-forward evaluation,
purged validation where labels overlap, realistic fees/spread/slippage/impact, and reproducible
inputs. A result is a candidate for shadow observation—not proof of future performance.
