# Risk management

Risk is a deterministic veto layer, not a narrative suggestion. Validate universe, freshness,
reserve floor, allocation bounds, turnover, rebalance frequency, data disagreement, volatility,
drawdown, pause state, and the global kill switch before any simulated action. A model can recommend
less exposure; it cannot expand authority, bypass a veto, or reinterpret a failed check as approval.
